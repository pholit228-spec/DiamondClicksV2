# DiamondClicks

Hold the jump button and every frame becomes a real click, like an autoclicker, instead of one long hold.

- **Click delay**: how many frames pass between clicks while holding (`1` = a click on every frame).
- **Gamemodes**: choose where it works (cube, ship, ball, UFO, wave, robot, spider, swing). For example only wave and ship.
- Everything can be switched off with the **Enabled** toggle.

Made by **diamondsoup**.
